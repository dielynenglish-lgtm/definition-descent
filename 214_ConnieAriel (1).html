Game Rules & Mechanics

Turret Defense: Your 3 turrets automatically lock onto and fire at enemies walking along the designated path toward your base.
10 Shots Ammo Limit: Each turret holds 10 shots. When a turret runs out of ammo, it stops firing and triggers a Vocabulary Puzzle in the sidebar.
Correct Answer: Clicking the correct vocabulary word instantly reloads ALL turrets to maximum ammo!
5-Second Lockdown Penalty: Clicking the wrong word locks that specific turret down for a 5-second penalty phase!
Base Integrity: Base health starts at 100% and drops by 10% each time an enemy breaches the base core on the right.
